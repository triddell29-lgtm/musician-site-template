Biography

A lifetime devoted to the classical guitar

Elias Marlowe has honed his craft under the guidance of renowned maestros around the world. His musical journey began at the conservatory, and his pursuit of excellence led him to study in Madrid, refining the Spanish tradition at its source.

He has since performed in concert halls, cathedrals, vineyards and private estates — and has taken part in masterclasses with some of the most celebrated guitarists of our time. Today he brings that same caliber of artistry to weddings, concerts, corporate events and the teaching studio.

FAQ

Good to know

Can't find your answer? Send a message.

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book 6–12 months ahead. Spring and fall weekends fill quickly, so reach out early to secure your date.

Can you learn a song that isn't in your repertoire?

Yes. Custom arrangements of special songs are available and can usually be prepared within a few weeks.

Do you provide your own sound equipment?

A professional, discreet PA system and wireless microphone for the officiant are available for most venues.

Do you travel outside Arizona?

Absolutely — destination weddings and touring concerts are welcome. Travel fees vary by location.
